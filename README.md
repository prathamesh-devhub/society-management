# Society Management System

A web-based Society Management System built with Laravel 12 to manage residential societies efficiently.

## Features

### Member Management

* Add, Edit, Delete Members
* Store contact details
* Profile photo support

### Complaint Management

* Register complaints
* Track complaint status
* Priority management

### Vehicle Management

* Manage member vehicles

### Tenant Management

* Manage tenant information

### Notice Board

* Create and publish society notices
* Expiry date support

### Maintenance Billing

* Generate monthly maintenance bills
* Auto calculation based on square feet
* Previous dues and advance adjustment
* Interest calculation

### Maintenance Payments

* Record payments
* Receipt number generation
* Outstanding balance tracking

### Expense Management

* Track society expenses
* Upload bill copies
* Categorize expenses

### Reports

* Defaulters Report
* Maintenance Reports
* Expense Reports

### PDF & Email

* Generate maintenance bills in PDF format
* Email bills with PDF attachments
* Bulk email support

## Technology Stack

* Laravel 12
* PHP 8.2
* MySQL
* Blade Templates
* Tailwind CSS
* DomPDF
* Mailtrap
